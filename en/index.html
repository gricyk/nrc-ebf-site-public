Vision

To be an effective instrument in God’s hands to help local NRC-EBF member churches, working together with national local Baptist and evangelical unions, bring people to saving faith and discipleship in Jesus Christ.

Mission

Our mission is aimed at Russian-speaking people from all over the world living on the European continent. We provide accessible resources to local churches and missions to:

  • proclaim the gospel of Jesus Christ;
  • promote Christian discipleship and mentoring as a way of life;
  • open new churches;
  • develop partnerships between evangelical churches and unions in Europe;
  • promote missions around the world.

Membership

Membership is open to all churches and groups that willingly seek to realize the mission, vision and doctrine based on the principles described in the NRC-EBF Constitution. To apply, contact the NRC-EBF coordinators.
